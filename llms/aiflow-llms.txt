## What this company does

aiFlow reads public sources — company websites, press, deal announcements and professional profiles
— to produce data-driven evaluations of C-level and board candidates. The platform covers executive
research, revenue estimates with a published comparable-company methodology, transaction and funding
history, backchannel and reference discovery, candidate pipeline and search management, AI interview
synthesis and scoring, and board-deck generation. Buyers are PE talent partners, firm leadership,
search firm partners, researchers and in-house recruiting teams.

Founded 2022 in San Francisco by Nick Manske and Josh Gardner. Y Combinator Winter 2023.

## Brand and entity

The aiFlow brand site remains live at https://aiflow.solutions/. The operating product today is

auth.aiflow.solutions, and the founders are the same.

## The one machine-readable surface

The company serves standard OpenID Connect and OAuth 2.0 authorization-server discovery documents on
its own Auth0 custom domain. These gate sign-in to the web application; they are not an API.

- https://auth.aiflow.solutions/.well-known/openid-configuration (HTTP 200)
- https://auth.aiflow.solutions/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server (HTTP 200)

Issuer: https://auth.aiflow.solutions/. Authorization code with PKCE (S256), client credentials,
refresh token, device code and token exchange grants. DPoP (ES256), dynamic client registration,
token revocation and MFA are advertised. Scopes are the standard OIDC identity set plus
offline_access — there are no product or resource scopes.
